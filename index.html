01 — THE INVITATION

An invitation.
A new perspective.

I was invited by AFRETEC and AfriSMAT to spend ten days in Kigali, Rwanda, exploring artificial intelligence and the technologies shaping our future.

Representing Sudan at the African Inclusive Digital Industries program was more than an opportunity to study. It was a chance to meet young people from across Africa, share ideas, and imagine how we can solve the challenges we face every day.

We learned, collaborated, explored, and challenged ourselves. I came home seeing technology—and the continent I call home—with fresh eyes.

THE TEAM. THE CERTIFICATE. THE MOMENT.

As part of the program, participants from different African countries were challenged to identify a real African problem and propose a technology-driven solution.

My team and I put everything into our project. What made us special wasn't that we all thought alike. We each brought a different background, a different perspective, and our own unfair advantage.

Together, those differences became our greatest strength. We challenged one another, built on each other's ideas, and became the team that took first place.
